# 🚗 Automotive Sales & Revenue Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze automotive sales and revenue performance across different companies, customer segments, vehicle types, engine types, colors, and time periods.

The dashboard focuses on turning raw sales data into clear business insights that can help understand **revenue performance, customer distribution, and product-level trends**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer practical business questions such as:

- Which car companies generate the highest revenue?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- Which vehicle types contribute the most revenue?
- Which car colors generate the highest revenue?
- How is revenue distributed between male and female customers?
- How do transmission, engine, company, gender, and year selections affect performance?

## 🔎 Key Insights

From the current dashboard view:

- **Oldsmobile** is the highest-revenue company at approximately **$14.8M**.
- **Volkswagen** and **Toyota** follow closely with approximately **$13.2M** and **$13.1M**.
- **SUVs** generate the highest revenue among vehicle types at approximately **$70M**.
- **Pale White** is the highest-revenue car color at approximately **$125M**.
- Monthly revenue shows noticeable variation, with strong peaks around **May and August**.
- Revenue is distributed across both male and female customer segments, with the male segment contributing the larger share in the displayed view.

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The project follows a typical analytics workflow:

1. Imported the automotive sales dataset into Power BI.
2. Reviewed and transformed the source data using Power Query.
3. Checked data types and prepared fields for analysis.
4. Created calculated measures/KPIs for business reporting.
5. Built interactive visuals and slicers.
6. Designed the final dashboard with a focus on readability and business usability.
